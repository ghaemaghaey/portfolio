About

Data processing workflows, web crawling systems, automation. Two-plus years of it, in production.

I'm Ghaem — a data engineer from Iran. My work sits between two places: the messy, unstructured public web and the ordered tables that a business actually queries. I write the crawlers that go get it, the workflows that clean and shape it, and the tooling that keeps the whole thing observable when something upstream changes its markup overnight.

Most of what I run is Python — FastAPI services, pyodbc against SQL Server, schedulers, queue workers. The front ends I build are Vue. I care about tools that work offline, on a laptop, without a login: air-gapped UIs, single executables, local-first apps. When something annoys me enough I open-source it.
